# Build Games Like the Pros

Would you like to develop your own 2D game? In this workshop, you’ll learn about the Godot game engine! Together, we’ll build a classic platformer with jumping characters, obstacles, and custom levels.
Some programming experience (e.g., with Scratch or another language) is very helpful! Suitable for children ages 10 and up.

We guide you through every step. Bring your ideas and bring your game to life!

Schedule:
10:50 - 11:00 - Admission
11:00 - 11:15 - Welcome & Ice-Breaker & Setup
11:15 - 12:45 - Programming
12:45 - 13:00 - Show time: who did what today?
