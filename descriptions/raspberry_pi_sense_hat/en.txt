# With Pixels and Sensors - Raspberry Pi

In this workshop, we will bring the Raspberry Pi to life!
Using the Sense Hat you will learn how to program your own pixel animations, flashing patterns, and funny icons.
Step by step, we’ll show you how to control the bright lights using the Python programming language and make your own ideas shine on the display.

No prior knowledge is strictly necessary – but if you already have a bit of experience with Scratch or Python, that's great.
Our mentors will support you every step of the way!

Curious? Then drop by and become a pixel artist!

For kids ages 9 to 17.
