# Minecraft with MCreator

In this workshop, children aged 9 and up learn how to create their own little mod for Minecraft – no programming experience required!
With MCreator, we use simple code blocks to create new items, creatures, or game mechanics. The whole thing is visual and playful.
MCreator is in English, so a little knowledge of English or support from parents is helpful.

Schedule:
10:50 - 11:00 - Admission
11:00 - 11:15 - Welcome & Ice-Breaker & Setup
11:15 - 12:45 - Programming
12:45 - 13:00 - Show time: who did what today?
