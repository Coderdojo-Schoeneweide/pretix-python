# Coding Games with AI

How does Artificial Intelligence actually work? In this workshop, we'll find out and quickly and easily create our very own game using the OpenCode tool!

First, we'll step into the shoes of an AI ourselves and discover why precise instructions are so crucial.
Next, we'll test your knowledge with a quick AI quiz before diving into your game: step by step, we will get your code up and running.

No prior experience is necessary. Our mentors will support you every step of the way! Curious? Come by and bring your ideas to life!
