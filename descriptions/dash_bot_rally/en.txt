# Dash Bot Rally

In this workshop, we bring the little Dash robot to life!
Together we master exciting challenges and learn step-by-step how to program Dash. Whether an obstacle course, theater, or slingshot—you decide where the journey goes.
You don't need any prior knowledge! Our mentors will support you through every challenge and show you how much fun robotics programming can be.

Recommended for children between 7 and 14 years old.

If you are under 13 years old: A legal guardian to accompany you during the Dojo. The accompanying adult also needs their own ticket. If the ticket combination your family needs is no longer available, just write to us and we'll see if we can find a solution.

### Accessibility
All areas of the Treptow Central Library are accessible to people with limited mobility. There is no tactile guidance system for the blind. Doors are sometimes heavy.
We generally offer our workshops in German (spoken and written). In addition, almost all mentors speak English.
If you need other languages (e.g., Russian, Arabic) or further support (e.g., screen readers), let us know. We try to make our workshops accessible to everyone.

### Food and Drinks
Anyone who'd like to is very welcome to bring a small snack or drink. We always try to set up a small buffet.
