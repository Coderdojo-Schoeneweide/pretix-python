# Understanding AI

Let's take a look behind the scenes of artificial intelligence! You'll learn how computers can “see” and understand images.
Together, we'll train and use a small AI that can distinguish between different objects or hand gestures.

No prior knowledge required.

An event presented by the Treptow-Köpenick Public Library in cooperation with CoderDojo Schöneweide e.V. as part of the “Understanding AI” event series.
