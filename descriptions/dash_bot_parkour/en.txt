# Robot Labyrinth

Note: This workshop will be held in the same format on Thursday and Friday. It's best to register for only one of the workshops so that tickets are available for others as well.

In this workshop, we will bring the clever robot Dash to life!
Together, we will program Dash for four exciting challenges: scoring goals in robot soccer, performing a short theatrical play, building a mechanical catapult,
and navigating a tricky maze.

No prior knowledge is required – you will learn how to give the robot commands in a fun, hands-on way. Our mentors will support you every step of the way!
Just bring your curiosity and plenty of enthusiasm.

For kids ages 5 to 13.

If you are under 13 years old: One:n legal guardian to accompany you during the dojo. The guardian also needs a separate ticket. If the combination of tickets your family needs is no longer available, just write us and we'll see if we can find a solution.

### Accessibility
All areas of the library in Schöneweide are accessible to people with limited mobility. There is no audio description system. Some doors can be difficult to open.
We generally offer our workshops in German (spoken and written). Almost all mentors also speak English.
If you need support in other languages (e.g., Russian, Arabic) or additional assistance (e.g., screen readers), please let us know. We strive to make our workshops accessible to everyone.

### Food
You are also welcome to bring something small to eat/drink. We always try to provide a small buffet.
