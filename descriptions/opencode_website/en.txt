# Create Your Own Website with AI

In this workshop, you'll learn how websites are built today – with smart support from AI!
Together, we will use OpenCode and Agentic Coding tools that act like a digital assistant, helping you write and design code.
We’ll show you how to turn your ideas into your very own website, add images and text, and let the AI help you solve problems along the way.
No prior knowledge is required – all you need is curiosity and a desire to experiment. Our mentors will support you every step of the way!

### What you need:
* A laptop for programming. If you don't have one, book an additional computer product or write to us. We'll find a solution!
* Paper and pens for your designs and notes.
