# A Book Becomes a Game

In this workshop series, we bring stories to life! We take a book as our template and program our own computer game from it step by step using Scratch.
Together, we will develop characters, design exciting levels, and interactively turn the plot into code.
Our mentors will support you every step of the way! No prior experience required.
Important: This is a sequential workshop series. It is best if you attend all sessions so we can continuously work on our game together.
Curious? Then come by and bring your imagination!

Hosted by the Stadtbibliothek Treptow-Köpenick in cooperation with CoderDojo Schöneweide e.V. and the Remili Youth Club as part of the "Gemeinsam digital" (Digital Together) event series.

Accessibility: All areas of the library in Schöneweide are accessible to people with limited mobility. There is no audio description system. Some doors can be difficult to open.
