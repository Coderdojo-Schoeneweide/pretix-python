# Workshop: Smart Trains – Coding with Intelino

In this workshop, we bring smart trains to life! With the Intelino smart train system, kids discover the fundamentals of coding in a playful way.
Together, we build our own rail networks and explore ways to control the trains.

No prior experience required. Our mentors are here to support you every step of the way!

Curious? Drop by and get your ideas rolling!
