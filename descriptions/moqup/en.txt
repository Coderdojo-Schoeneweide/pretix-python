# Create Your Own Website

In this workshop, you will become designers and create your own website—entirely without coding!
Using the online tool Moqups, you will work in teams of two to build a clickable "mockup": a preview image of a website complete with buttons, menus, and images.

First, we will play a fun game all about "What I mean vs. what people hear," and then you will receive a real design assignment.

No prior knowledge is needed—curiosity is more than enough! Our mentors will help you every step of the way. Come on by and bring your wildest app ideas with you!
