In this workshop, we will explore how to program music, paint pictures, or control small virtual robots using a computer.

Since we work directly with text-based code, this workshop is ideal if you already have some initial experience—for example, with Scratch or other platforms. Of course, we are here to help!

If you are under 13 years old:
One legal guardian to accompany you during the dojo. The guardian also needs a separate ticket. If the combination of tickets your family needs is no longer available, just write us and we'll see if we can find a solution.

Accessibility: All areas of the library in Schöneweide are accessible to people with limited mobility. There is no audio description system. Some doors can be difficult to open.
We generally offer our workshops in German (spoken and written). Almost all mentors also speak English.
If you need support in other languages (e.g., Russian, Arabic) or additional assistance (e.g., screen readers), please let us know. We strive to make our workshops accessible to everyone.
