In this workshop, children and teens will dive into creative teamwork: Together, we’ll develop our own Pokémon or another imaginative character—step by step and drawing on lots of ideas from the group.
To do this, we’ll use Miro, a digital whiteboard where we can draw, write, collect, and visualize our ideas together. The result will be a large shared board filled with sketches, characteristics, names, abilities, and stories.
No prior experience is necessary—all you need is a desire for creativity and teamwork. Our mentors will help you get started and support you in bringing your ideas to life.
Do you want to create something of your own and build an entire fantasy world together with others? Then join us!

Accessibility: All areas of the library in Schöneweide are accessible to people with limited mobility. There is no audio description system. Some doors can be difficult to open.
We generally offer our workshops in German (spoken and written). Almost all mentors also speak English.
If you need support in other languages (e.g., Russian, Arabic) or additional assistance (e.g., screen readers), please let us know. We strive to make our workshops accessible to everyone.

You are also welcome to bring something small to eat/drink. We always try to provide a small buffet.
