# Free Coding & Training for the "Informatik-Biber" competition

At our Free Coding event, kids and teens can bring their own projects and work on them with the support of our mentors.
You don't have an idea yet? No problem, we bring inspiration :) Previous knowledge is not required.

For kids in 3rd grade and up: You can also take part in the [Informatik-Biber](https://bwinf.de/biber/2026/) competition, which features short logic puzzles.

Schedule:
10:50 - 11:00 - Admission
11:00 - 11:15 - Welcome & Ice-Breaker & Setup
11:15 - 12:45 - Programming
12:45 - 13:00 - Show time: who did what today?
