# MBot Labyrinth

This workshop is all about robots, logic, and fun challenges! Using the small learning robots called mBot, we’ll program clever strategies to navigate through a maze.
You’ll discover how sensors work, how to give basic commands, and how to solve problems step by step – in a playful and creative way!

No previous experience needed – we’ll teach you everything you need to know. If you’ve worked with Scratch before, you’ll have an easier start.
Our mentors are always there to help!

Ready for a robot adventure? Come join us and bring your curiosity!
