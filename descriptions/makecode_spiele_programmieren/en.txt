# Game Programming with MakeCode

In this workshop, you will design and develop your very own retro video game! Using Microsoft MakeCode Arcade, you'll learn how to control characters, collect points, build levels, and bring your game worlds to life—using either visual code blocks or real text-based code.

No prior experience is necessary. Our mentors are always by your side to help you turn your game ideas into reality.

Accessibility: All areas of the library in Schöneweide are accessible to people with limited mobility. There is no audio description system. Some doors can be difficult to open.
